Stop 4 · Timekeepers

Gallery 4 · Chronos Corridor Duration: ~3:15 Featuring: Horologist Theo Maren

Hundreds of meticulously synchronized clocks tick in unison before gradually diverging. Timekeepers examines the fragile balance between collective schedules and personal rhythms through light, sound, and motion.

Key Details

  • Medium: Mechanical clocks, projected timelines, ambient drone composition.
  • Technique: Clocks are reset nightly by hand to begin in sync.
  • Listen for: Choral voices fading in as the clocks drift apart.

Reflection Prompts

  • When does the ticking begin to feel dissonant rather than unified?
  • Which clock draws your attention when the synchronization dissolves?
