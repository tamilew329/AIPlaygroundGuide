How to Navigate the Exhibition with This Audio Guide

Welcome, explorers. The "Echoes of Time" audio guide accompanies an exhibition that traces how artists capture the rhythm of history, technology, and human memory. Each stop is designed to be experienced in sequence, yet the guide allows you to jump to pieces that resonate with you most.

Before You Begin

  • Grab a pair of headphones for an immersive experience.
  • Each narration lasts between 2 and 4 minutes.
  • Look for the exhibit number on the gallery label to match the audio track.

What to Expect

The index on the next page presents all six exhibition stops. Tap or click any stop to hear its story. You can pause, resume, and rewind each audio track as you take in the artwork. When you are ready, use the link below to open the index.

Take your time. Let each piece speak to you, then allow the narration to fill in the hidden layers, connections, and the historical echoes that inspired the curator to bring this exhibition together.

Ready to continue? Head to the index to select your first stop.
