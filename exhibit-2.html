Stop 2 · Kinetic Memories

Gallery 2 · Motion Hall Duration: ~2:45 Featuring: Artist Amara Feld

Suspended ribbons of copper and glass store fleeting gestures as they sway. Sensors translate the movement into short melodic phrases, capturing the physical memory of each visitor who passes through.

Key Details

  • Medium: Copper filaments, glass, custom sensors, live generative audio.
  • Interactive: The installation logs its recordings to an open archive updated nightly.
  • Listen for: Layered harmonies created from the previous hour’s visitors.

Reflection Prompts

  • What gesture would you leave behind to mark this moment?
  • How do the sounds shift as more people move through the work?
