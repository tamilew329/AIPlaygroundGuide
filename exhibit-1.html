Stop 1 · Dawn of Signals

Gallery 1 · Telegraph Chamber Duration: ~3:05 Featuring: Curator Lina Ortega

Step into the telegraph chamber, where artist Kamal Singh layers antique transmitters with contemporary light sculptures. The narration explores how early long-distance communication altered our sense of presence and urgency.

Key Details

  • Medium: Mixed-media installation with kinetic light arrays.
  • Created: 2018, collection of the National Museum of Signals.
  • Listen for: Morse code phrases embedded within the low-frequency tones.

Reflection Prompts

  • How does waiting for the pulses of light shift your sense of time?
  • What modern messages would you send if you had only a telegraph key?
