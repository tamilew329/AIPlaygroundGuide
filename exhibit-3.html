Stop 3 · Resonant Voices

Gallery 3 · Oral History Alcove Duration: ~3:30 Featuring: Community Archive Collective

Immerse yourself in multi-channel recordings sourced from intergenerational interviews. Resonant Voices highlights the cadence, pauses, and laughter that shape cultural memory in the city’s historic river district.

Key Details

  • Medium: Nine-channel audio, responsive LEDs, archival photography.
  • Collaborators: The River District Oral History Society.
  • Listen for: Ambient sounds captured on location interwoven with interviews.

Reflection Prompts

  • Which voice or cadence stays with you after leaving the alcove?
  • How do the surrounding visual elements frame the storytelling?
