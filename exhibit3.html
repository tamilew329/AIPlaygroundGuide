About This Work

This Renaissance portrait demonstrates the era's revolutionary approach to depicting the human form. The subtle play of light and shadow (chiaroscuro) creates a sense of depth and volume that brings the subject to life.

Historical Context

During the Renaissance, portraiture evolved from stiff, formal representations to intimate psychological studies. This work captures not just the physical appearance but the inner character of the sitter, a hallmark of Renaissance humanism.

Period: Italian Renaissance
Medium: Oil on Wood Panel
Technique: Chiaroscuro
Dimensions: 77 × 53 cm
