About This Work

This magnificent sculpture exemplifies the technical mastery and artistic vision of the Hellenistic period. The flowing drapery and dynamic pose capture a moment of movement, frozen in marble for eternity.

Historical Context

Created during an era of great cultural exchange, this sculpture reflects the Hellenistic emphasis on emotion and naturalism. The artist's ability to render fabric and human form in stone remains unparalleled even by modern standards.

Period: Hellenistic (323-31 BCE)
Medium: Marble
Origin: Ancient Greece
Height: 2.44 meters
