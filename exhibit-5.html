Stop 5 · Digital Tapestry

Gallery 5 · Immersive Atrium Duration: ~4:00 Featuring: Technologist Priya Raman

Digital Tapestry reimagines weaving through projected strands of light fed by live climate data. The audio track unravels how the artist bridges ancient textile techniques with contemporary coding practices.

Key Details

  • Medium: Large-scale projection mapping, hand-dyed textiles, environmental datasets.
  • Data Source: Live satellite feeds tracking ocean temperatures and wind speed.
  • Listen for: Subtle harp motifs derived from historical weaving patterns.

Reflection Prompts

  • How do digital and handmade textures meet within the installation?
  • What climate story emerges as the projected colors shift?
