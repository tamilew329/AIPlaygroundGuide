Listen to Audio Description 3:45

About This Work

This remarkable post-impressionist painting captures the swirling energy of the night sky. The artist's bold brushstrokes and vibrant blues create a sense of movement and emotion that transcends the simple representation of a starry night.

Historical Context

Created during a period of intense artistic innovation, this work represents a departure from traditional landscape painting. The artist sought to express the emotional and spiritual dimensions of the natural world rather than merely reproduce its appearance.

Period: Late 19th Century
Medium: Oil on Canvas
Style: Post-Impressionism
Dimensions: 73.7 × 92.1 cm
