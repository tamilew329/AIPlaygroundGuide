Stop 6 · Echo Chamber

Gallery 6 · Reflection Studio Duration: ~3:50 Featuring: Visitor Voices Collective

The final stop invites you to contribute your own reflection. Echo Chamber blends live recordings from visitors with a responsive sound field that resonates beneath the gallery’s domed ceiling, creating a shared sonic archive.

Key Details

  • Medium: Interactive microphones, spatial audio array, architectural acoustics.
  • Participation: Add your voice at the recording station as you exit.
  • Listen for: Harmonic overtones created by the room’s natural resonance.

Reflection Prompts

  • What insight will you leave for future listeners?
  • How do the voices layer together as more contributions are added?
